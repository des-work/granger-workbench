# Frutiger Metro  (design profile: `frutiger-metro`)

**Era:** c. 2004–2012 — Web 2.0, vector-Flash and phone-box culture

*Frutiger Aero's flat 2D sibling (mid-2000s vector culture): loud magenta/lime/aqua/orange color blocks, bloom flowers and flourishes, human silhouettes, dot constellations and speech bubbles on clean white — humanist type, rich gradients inside flat shapes, zero skeuomorphic gloss.*

## Origins

Mid-2000s vector/Flash culture: music-player skins, mobile-carrier ads, club flyers and Web-2.0 branding, catalogued by CARI as the flat 2D sibling of Frutiger Aero — where Aero rendered glossy water and glass, Metro drew the same humanist optimism as crisp vector blooms and dancing silhouettes.

## Composition & space

Motifs cluster in a corner and BLEED off the edge — a dense flourish zone against a big calm color field. Diagonal energy carries the eye across; generous white (or one flat field) keeps the loud colors legible. Never wallpaper the whole surface.

## Proportion & measure

Motif clusters claim 25-35% of the canvas, anchored to a corner. Dot constellations fade along a curve, each dot about 0.75x the last. Swooshes span the full width on 15-25 degree diagonals. Rounded rects at roughly one-sixth of their height; white keeps at least half the field.

## Color logic

Full-spectrum saturated jumps — magenta to lime to aqua to orange to violet — sitting on clean white. Gradients are smooth hue sweeps that live INSIDE shapes (a petal shifts pink→orange); backgrounds stay flat. Black is rare and soft (slate), never harsh.

## Light

No diegetic light at all — but plenty of GLOW: a soft radial bloom behind motif clusters like a dance-floor wash. Whites stay bright, nothing casts a shadow, nothing reflects; luminosity is drawn, not simulated.

## Typography

Rounded humanist sans in the Frutiger/Segoe lineage, friendly and lowercase-leaning, often framed in speech bubbles or riding a swoosh. No serifs, no distress, no letter-spaced shouting — it talks like a helpful kiosk.

## Materiality & finish

Pure vector — the crisp anti-aliased edge IS the material. At most a soft glow behind a motif. No bevels, no gloss, no photo texture: flatness here is a positive quality, not a missing feature.

## Motion & rhythm

Growth and cascade: vines and blooms draw themselves, dot constellations pour in one after another, silhouettes dance mid-jump. Flash-tween smoothness — energetic, continuous, never jittery.

## Imagery

Human silhouettes mid-dance or mid-leap, filled with gradients or patterns rather than detail; vector birds and butterflies; no photography at all — people are shapes with joy, not faces.

## Voice (copy & tone)

Friendly kiosk optimism: lowercase, inclusive 'we', one exclamation point allowed — 'share your world!' Technology promises phrased as feelings; nothing ironic, nothing edgy, everything invitational.

## Sound

Club-lite electro and airy synth stabs with ringtone-era gloss; UI sounds bright and short, like drawn bubbles popping. The soundtrack of a 2007 phone ad where everyone is dancing.

## Where it lives

Phone-box packaging, carrier ads, music-player skins, event flyers, mall kiosks and early social-web branding — surfaces selling connection itself.

## Kinship (family tree)

THE defining relationship: flat 2D sibling of `frutiger-aero` — Aero renders glossy water and glass, Metro draws the same humanist optimism in vectors. Cousin of vectordelia. Don't confuse with Microsoft's Metro UI — that's typographic austerity; this is decorative bloom.

## Hierarchy of power

Motif energy > hue jumps > silhouette > type. There is no texture tier at all — remove the blooms and dots and nothing is left, which is exactly the point.

## Canon (study these)

Study: the dopplr identity, last.fm-era gig flyers, Nokia and T-Mobile carrier ads c. 2006, Winamp and WMP skin culture, Vodafone's speechmark years, and the iPod silhouette campaign (the crossover hit).

## Adaptation (modes & accessibility)

Magenta on white clears 5:1 — body-safe. Lime is decorative ONLY (under 2:1); all running text sits in slate. Period-authentic dark mode barely existed — do 'club mode' instead: deep violet field, motifs glowing brighter, white type. Being vector-native it survives any DPI unharmed.

## Signature moves

1. Cluster a bloom flourish in one corner and bleed it off-edge
2. Run a smooth hue sweep INSIDE each petal; keep the field flat
3. Cascade a dot constellation that fades as it travels
4. Add one dancing silhouette filled with pattern, not detail

## Components (this vault)

- **tokens/style**: `frutiger-metro` (`assets kit frutiger-metro` for a full stylesheet)

- **fonts**: display: Fredoka, body: Poppins
- **textures (native)**: halftone, mesh
- **gloss**: flat-vivid: no bevels or glass; energy comes from saturated fills and gradient sweeps inside crisp vector shapes
- **shapes**: bloom-flower, metro-dots, swoosh-ribbon
- **patterns**: dots, wave-divider
- **techniques (fx)**: holographic-conic, svg-soft-glow, sheen-sweep

**Rules:** DO cluster motifs into corners and let them bleed off-edge, use full-spectrum hue jumps. DON'T add 3D depth, drop shadows or photo textures — Aero does that; Metro is flat.

**You've lost the plot when:** Ruined by importing Aero's gloss — aqua bubbles, 3D bevels, water droplets — or by photo textures and dark grunge. If it stops looking like it shipped on a 2006 phone box, it's drifted.
