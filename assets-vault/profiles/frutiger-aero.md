# Frutiger Aero  (design profile: `frutiger-aero`)

**Era:** c. 2004–2013 — Vista/PSP-era techno-optimism

*2004-13 techno-optimist humanism: glossy water, glass, grass and sky. Bubbles rise, koi drift, interfaces float as translucent panes over bright ecosystems — technology and nature presented as best friends, sincerely.*

## Origins

Named (by the CARI community, c. 2017) after Adrian Frutiger, whose humanist typefaces captioned the era: Windows Vista/7 Aero glass, PSP and Wii packaging, bottled-water and airline ads, eco-tech marketing. The optimistic prelude to flat design — the future as a clean bright morning.

## Composition & space

Horizon-based: airy sky above, grounded detail below, content floating mid-air in glass panels. Circular elements — orbs, bubbles, lens glints — drift through the upper field. Depth comes from stacked translucency, not shadow; everything gets air around it.

## Proportion & measure

Glass panels rounded 12-20px; bubble clusters of 3-7 spanning three scales; the specular highlight claims the top 30-45% of any glossy object. Horizon sits at the lower third. Whitespace is literal air — crowding breaks the weather.

## Color logic

Sky blues, aqua cyans and grass greens with white light everywhere; gradients behave like light through water. Saturation is high but nature-calibrated — never neon. A warm accent (koi orange) appears rarely and reads as precious.

## Light

Everything is backlit by a friendly sun: specular highlights, caustic shimmer, lens glints on glass edges. Light sources sit above-and-behind; whites bloom softly. Nothing is ever underlit or moody — the weather is permanently 10am.

## Typography

Humanist sans (the Frutiger/Segoe lineage), sentence case, friendly and unforced — often white with a soft glow when set over imagery. No serifs, no condensed shouting, no distress; the type is a clear announcement in a bright terminal.

## Materiality & finish

Water, glass, dew, polished plastic, wet grass. Every surface is clean and slightly wet — the wet look IS the aesthetic. Surfaces reflect and refract; dryness reads as broken.

## Motion & rhythm

Buoyant drift: bubbles rise, light sweeps across glass, clouds parallax slowly. Easing behaves like water — smooth, continuous, gently damped. Nothing snaps or bounces; things surface.

## Imagery

Macro water droplets, koi and dolphins, grass blades against blue sky, wind turbines, kids mid-jump — stock-photo optimism rendered pristine and saturated. The camera loves condensation.

## Voice (copy & tone)

Bright kiosk warmth with an eco-promise: 'technology in harmony with nature.' Simple present tense, inclusive and sincere — this is the pre-irony internet speaking, and it genuinely believes the future will be nice.

## Sound

Airy pads, water-drop UI sounds, glassy chimes — the Vista startup chord is the national anthem. Everything sounds hydrated and slightly reverberant, like a bright atrium.

## Where it lives

OS chrome and default wallpapers, device packaging, airport ad lightboxes, bottled-water labels, hopeful institutional PowerPoints, early smart-home concepts — surfaces selling clean futures.

## Kinship (family tree)

Parent of `frutiger-metro` (its flat 2D sibling — Aero renders the gloss Metro draws); grandparent of modern glassmorphism; cousin of `y2k-chrome` (shared optimism — Y2K chromes it, Aero hydrates it). Don't confuse with vaporwave, which mourns this era ironically; Aero lives it sincerely.

## Hierarchy of power

Light and gloss > color ecology > glass depth > imagery > type. If the surfaces stop glinting, no arrangement of the rest will bring the morning back.

## Canon (study these)

Study: Windows Vista/7 Aero UI and wallpaper suites, PSP packaging, Fiji and Evian label eras, ANA/Singapore Airlines ads c. 2008, the glossy-orb icon culture of 2006 — and this vault's own frutiger-aero default theme.

## Adaptation (modes & accessibility)

White type over imagery needs a glow or scrim; body ink stays deep sea-blue on the light field (tokens are contrast-audited). Dark mode is 'aero night': deep-ocean blues, bioluminescent accents, glass still glossy. Reduced motion: stop the drift, keep the gloss.

## Signature moves

1. Glass panel with a top sheen floating over a sky-to-grass scene
2. Bubble cluster rising through one corner at three scales
3. One glossy orb as the hero object, highlight in its upper third
4. Horizon at the lower third with a white glow at the seam

**Rules:** DO stack translucent layers, keep nature in frame, light from above-behind. DON'T flatten (that's Metro), don't neon (that's cyberpunk), don't wink (that's vaporwave).

**You've lost the plot when:** Dry equals dead: the moment surfaces stop being wet and backlit it collapses into generic corporate blue. Equally fatal: irony, darkness, or flattening the gloss away.
